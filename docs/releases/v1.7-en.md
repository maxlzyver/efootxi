## V1.6 highlights

- Added the 48-team World Cup, 24-team European Championship and 36-team Champions League. In all three cups, you can pick a team, use eligible cards you own, and view fixtures, standings, top scorers and assists.
- Added LAN Friend Match. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi, then create a room or enter a room code to play with your own squads.
- Improved promotion, relegation, next-season fixtures and season history in My League. Master League Sprint added manager signings with Sprint Points and revised cohesion, match points and season rewards.
- Added or restored 78 official player cards, added 25 DIY player cards and two DIY managers, and improved standard manager signing.

## What's new in V1.7

Upgrading from 1.6 to 1.7.

1. **Master League Sprint**
   Fixed a network error when changing managers and a bug that caused newly signed managers to disappear when re-entering the event. The Game Plan, squad and fixtures screens now show the same cohesion value for the upcoming match.

2. **Cups**
   The World Cup, European Championship and Champions League now check squad eligibility using the official event rules. If your squad is ineligible, the game explains what to change before kick-off.
   Knockout ties that need a winner now proceed to extra time and penalties as required by the format. The fixture list includes completed matches, and the round overview shows the actual matchups and path through the bracket. The goals and assists tables include both the group or league phase and the knockout rounds.
   After elimination, you can choose a team and start a new run. Eligible teams can also take an existing participant’s place. AI teams pick eligible cards from the full catalogue and select each player only once.

3. **Stadiums and Pack Previews**
   Fixed custom stadium settings failing to apply in some home matches. Pack previews now use the game's rating calculation to correct inflated ratings.

4. **Player Data and Badges**
   We checked 47,218 official player records, including appearance data, base attributes and initial boosters. Club and league affiliations were added to 1,915 national-team cards. The World Cup Big Time Messi card is now linked to Miami BP, and the World Cup Big Time Haaland card is linked to Manchester City.
   Corrected nine Borussia Dortmund player affiliations, restored the original 23-player PFA Team of the Year squad, and restored Scottish Premiership team data.

5. **Cards and Rewards**
   Restored 71 missing cards, including all 11 Magical Dribblers cards. You can find the restored cards by name in the standard GP player catalogue.
   The November 12 update adds 196 official cards. The Newest pool gains 119 special cards, and 77 Standard cards join the standard GP player catalogue. All 33 Big Time, Epic and Show Time cards from this batch go directly to your player collection.
   Updated 57 existing cards, including 56 Standard cards and one Highlight card.
   Added 13 official managers. The classic Arsenal version of Arsène Wenger and Mikel Arteta go directly to your manager collection. The other 11 are available through standard manager signing.
   The permanent pools now display ST01, ST02 and ST03 together, followed by the Epic pools.

## Installation

Requires Android 9 or later and a 64-bit ARM device. Install efootxi-1.7.apk over your existing 1.6 installation to keep your save. Stay online on the first launch and wait for the game to prepare the required resources.
