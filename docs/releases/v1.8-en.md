## What's new

1. **Fixed a bug that prevented manually played matches from starting in Master League Sprint.**
2. **Change the difficulty at any point in the three cups.** The World Cup, European Championship, and Champions League start on Legend difficulty. Choose from seven levels in Event Settings. Your choice takes effect in the next match and is remembered.
3. **Restart a cup and pick a team again.** Restart from Event Settings during a run, after going out in the group or knockout stage, or after winning the cup. Confirming takes you straight to team selection.
4. **Group Overview is now Competition Overview.** The group stage, Champions League league phase, and knockout stage share one in-game entry. The Overview tab shows your progress and next match. Open the Fixtures, Standings, Knockout, and Players tabs to see scores, the bracket, goal and assist leaderboards, and individual match details. Fixed a crash when opening the previous overview.
5. **Updated knockout details and trophy celebrations.** The details screen shows the round, fixture, and score, with the empty reward placeholders cleared. The game now gives priority to the trophy celebration after a final win in play or on penalties. The World Cup has its own scene. The European Championship and Champions League use the general trophy scene.
6. **Cup opponents build their strongest eligible squads from the full player catalogue.** The game draws a formation and team playstyle first, then selects eligible club or national-team players. For players with multiple cards, it compares their developed ratings at the position being filled and uses those ratings to build the starting XI and bench. The coach has **90** proficiency in all six team playstyles.
7. **AI Division builds opponents at matchmaking.** It draws a base team from 258 clubs and 159 national teams for the badge and kits. It then draws from 11 standard formations and six team playstyles, selects 23 different players by position from the full catalogue, and assigns a coach with 90 proficiency in every playstyle. **A squad qualifies with a Team Strength of 3,300 or above.** Player candidates are indexed by position and rating to shorten matchmaking.
8. **AI substitutions are set to the earliest setting.** This applies to AI Division, cups, My League, and Master League. Fixed a cup lineup bug that could disable automatic substitutions. AI starters can now be rotated while they still have stamina left.
9. **Fixed LAN multiplayer issues and improved match-start stability.**
10. **Synced the November 19 official match engine and Live Update ratings.** Updated 3,388 existing Live Update ratings and added 530 entries. Simulated matches use the same weekly ratings. Also synced rosters, formations, and tactics, added 62 base-player records, and updated 133 player records and 33 appearance records. Added 12 coach records and updated 10.
11. **This week's Newest adds 55 official cards.** All 29 Trending, 17 Highlight, seven Show Time, and two Epic cards are available through GP signing. A copy of each of the seven Show Time and two Epic cards is sent to Player Storage. Real Madrid's David Beckham has a maximum level of **35**, and Bayern's Oliver Kahn has a maximum level of **27**.
12. **All 197 cards from the previous Newest remain available through GP signing.** One Big Time moves to **BT 01**, 10 Show Time cards move to **ST 03**, and 32 Epic cards move to **Epic 03**. The other 154 cards, made up of 121 Highlight and 33 Trending cards, leave the draw pools and remain available through direct GP signing. Existing draw records are retained.
13. **Added 15 DIY cards.** Four Epic and 11 Big Time cards have a B Live Update rating. A copy of each is sent to Player Storage on first distribution, and all are available through GP signing. The DIY pool grows from **51 to 66 cards**. Nine new cards have exclusive boosters, including Li Huitang's “King of the Far East,” Gu Guangming's “Loach on the Byline,” and 2015 Messi's “King of Camp Nou.”
14. **Fixed an issue that let different cards of the same player appear in the same lineup.**
15. **Updated the Player Editor, development, card art, and Player Storage.** The editor now has Training, Weak Foot, and Playing Styles tabs, with search for playing styles. The additional skill limit is now **20**, and Stamina +6 joins the selectable boosters. DIY Big Time Henry's Live Update rating is now B. Corrected artwork on several new cards and removed the front border from DIY 2015 Messi. The mistakenly distributed copy of the weekly Jackson card was removed. Players keep any copies they signed themselves, and the card is still available through GP signing.
16. **Corrected club badges on national-team themed cards by card ID.** Reverted 2,020 overrides from 1.7 that used the current clubs on standard cards, then checked 3,691 full card IDs. Official records for 465 of those exact cards contained a club. Removed the incorrect Miami badge on 2014 World Cup Messi and the incorrect Milan badge on 2022 World Cup Modrić.
17. **Updated the app icon, home-screen interactions, and resource downloads.**

## Installation

Requires Android 9 or later and a 64-bit ARM device. If you already have 1.7, install efootxi-1.8.apk over your existing app to keep your save. Stay online on first launch, choose a patch or continue with your previous one, and wait for resource setup to finish before entering the game.
