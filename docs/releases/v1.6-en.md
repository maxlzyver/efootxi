## What's new

1. **Champions League mode**
   Uses the 36-team format for 2026/27. Each team plays eight matches in the league phase. The top eight go straight to the round of 16, teams ranked 9th–24th enter the knockout play-offs, and teams ranked 25th–36th are eliminated. From the play-offs through the semi-finals, ties are played over two legs. The final is a single match.

2. **World Cup mode**
   Uses the 48-team format for 2026. The top two in each of the 12 groups and the eight best third-placed teams reach the round of 32. AI-controlled Argentina uses the 2022 Argentina DIY squad, managed by Lionel Scaloni.

3. **EURO mode**
   Uses the 24-team format for 2024. The top two in each of the six groups and the four best third-placed teams reach the round of 16. The knockout rounds are single matches.

4. **LAN Friend Match**
   Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi and open Friend Match → Dream Team 1v1. One player creates a match room and the other joins with its room number. Both can use their own squads. The host can set the match format, duration, and condition for both sides. This version also adds LAN compatibility for HarmonyOS devices.

5. **Custom teams and player cards in all three cups**
   Open Match → Events. An eligible team can replace a team in the tournament field, so you can play the World Cup with China, for example. Use the squad and manager provided by the event or bring in cards you own. National-team eligibility follows player nationality. Club eligibility follows the club association of the specific card. Each cup saves its progress independently, and you can pick a new team after completing an edition. The default difficulty is Legend and can be changed before kick-off.

6. **Full cup fixtures and statistics**
   Select View All on the event home screen to browse the full tournament fixtures. Competition overview includes standings, the knockout bracket, top scorers, and assist leaders. Select a fixture to view the score, scorers, and assists, including for matches between AI teams. The report displays one language at a time. Use Language in the top-right corner to switch.

7. **Improved My League season settlement**
   Fixed a false relegation message when the league title was secured early. Leagues with promotion and relegation use the final standings to determine which teams move. The next season is scheduled with the updated team list. Previous results are available in season history.

8. **Updated AI squads and simulated results**
   My League uses updated base-team rosters. When multiple cards for the same player are eligible for that team, the card with a higher rating at the required position takes priority. Cup AI builds squads from eligible players by position and can vary formation and tactics between fixtures. Simulated results between AI teams take both lineups' strength into account.

9. **Updated Master League Sprint recruitment**
   New saves start with Julian Randall. Recruit players from the club, free-agent, and Standard lists, or spend Sprint Points to sign a manager. Player prices reflect ability and card type. Manager prices reflect tactical proficiency and special abilities.

10. **Updated Sprint cohesion, points, and season rewards**
    Cohesion reflects the starting lineup, positional proficiency, manager, stamina, and morale. The gap in cohesion is the main factor in simulated outcomes. Game Plan and the schedule display the values used by the simulation. Base match points are 20% higher, with the actual award based on difficulty, result, and relative team strength. On completing a season, the champion earns 5,000 Sprint Points, second and third each earn 3,000, and all other teams that finish earn 1,500.

11. **AI Division phases extend by 1,000 days after expiry**
    Existing points, divisions, and match history carry forward.

12. **Official weekly data synced**
    Updated base-team rosters, formations, and A–E Live Update ratings. All versions of Kylian Mbappé have an E rating under the project's rules.

13. **78 official player cards added or completed**
    The batch is available in Newest and the standard GP player catalogue. It includes Show Time Danny Welbeck and Epic versions of Frank Lampard, Paul Scholes, and Steven Gerrard. Eligible cards are automatically granted once under the collection gift rules.

14. **25 DIY player cards and two DIY managers added**
    The cards include Blue Lock versions of Virgil van Dijk and Filippo Inzaghi, a Ballon d'Or-themed Harry Kane, 2008 Cristiano Ronaldo, Fan Zhiyi, Sun Jihai, Gianni Rivera, Ali Daei, Gunnar Nordahl, and Alberto Spencer. Arsène Wenger and Alex Ferguson join the manager list. Wenger's card includes his Arsenal affiliation. The regular DIY draws are merged into one 51-card pool and retain existing draw history. The 2022 Argentina squad has a separate draw.

15. **Selectable Finishing +6 booster and missing skills**
    Finishing +6 is available as a selectable booster. Snap Strike has been added to the applicable versions of Andriy Shevchenko, Francesco Totti, Adriano, and Ruud Gullit.

16. **Player artwork and team data fixes**
    Added missing star ratings and animated artwork for some new cards. Corrected nationality and national-team or club badge errors, including Thibaut Courtois's Belgium affiliation and the 2018 DIY Cristiano Ronaldo card's Portugal affiliation. Also corrected some team abbreviations in the authenticity patches.

17. **Improved standard manager shop**
    Added missing manager versions and updated club affiliations. Fixed playstyle filters, sorting, and GP signings.

18. **Resource updates, startup compatibility, and display improvements**
    The launcher can resume an interrupted resource update and retrieve missing files. Improved startup on some Android emulators that previously showed a black screen or failed to enter the game. Main cup titles and labels follow the in-game language. The home carousel displays the full GitHub and Telegram addresses and promotes LAN Friend Match. When a newer version is available, V1.6 shows an update notice at online startup and lets you continue into the game.

## Upgrading from an older version

Install V1.6 over V1.4, V1.4.1, or V1.5 to keep your players, progression, squads, and league saves. Connect to the internet on the first launch after updating and follow the prompts to prepare your selected patch and new resources. Downloads can resume after an interruption. Once the resources are ready, single-player modes can be played offline. Both devices must run the same version for LAN matches.
