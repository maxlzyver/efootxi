## Update contents

1. Fixed the AI Division skip button being unusable.
2. Fixed a network error when crafting a booster in a single Featured-player booster slot.
3. Fixed duplicated booster slots and refresh problems after crafting a booster.
4. Restored permanent special and single-attribute boosters such as The King and The Village's Hope, and added Aerial Defending +4; conditional versions that depend on form or team requirements are not included.
5. Fixed display problems on some Show Time card art and animated backgrounds.
6. Fixed the black screen at startup caused by the Google Play resource interface error (-6).
7. Improved compatibility on devices that crashed after launch or a black screen.
8. Added the Original patch option while keeping the project's current badges and real team names.
9. Fixed the Champions League intro animation leaking into the Premier League and Original patches; each patch now plays its own intro.
10. Optimised cache cleanup after resource installation and patch switching, keeping saves, current resources and unfinished downloads. Storage use drops from over 10 GB to about 6 GB.
11. Fixed an incompatible resource index and engine format in the Original patch that crashed while loading badges in My League.
12. Fixed downloads that did not resume after an interrupted connection; the retry now shows the connection state and distinguishes whether the system network is available.
13. Optimised local initialisation to reduce temporary memory for small-file decryption and repeated save backups, while keeping resource authentication and save protection.
14. Fixed missing small portraits for DIY Cristiano Ronaldo and Ronaldo Nazário in My League scorer lists and similar lists.

## Upgrading

The package name and signing key are unchanged, so the new build can be installed over the previous one and existing saves are kept. Do not uninstall the app or clear its data first.

The first launch needs a network connection to download the selected patch resources (about 2.78 GB for Original, 2.87 GB for Champions League and 2.85 GB for Premier League). Wi-Fi is recommended. An interrupted download keeps its progress and continues afterwards.

Patches: this release provides Original, Champions League and Premier League; Original is selected by default. World Cup resources are not available yet.
