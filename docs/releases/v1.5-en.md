## r1525 fix

- Fixed the C_RTMC_9999 network error when starting AI Division or My League matches; those modes are no longer captured by the Sprint kick-off route.

## What's new

1. Following Kylian Mbappé's seriously inappropriate remarks, every Kylian Mbappé player card is fixed at E form. Routine live form updates will not change this setting.
2. Inzaghi's Offensive Awareness +6 booster is available as a selectable booster. Boosters supplied with DIY cards remain on those cards and are not added to the ordinary empty-slot selection.
3. The Player Editor can now change a player's playing style. Issues with applying changes, saving and booster slots have been fixed.
4. Player name search in Game Plan has been fixed. Search in the GP player contract shop now displays the matching player versions.
5. Fixed some player-specific animations and motions.
6. Added a separate “2022 Argentina” DIY pack before the regular DIY pack. Scaloni is added directly to the manager inventory. New players start with his Argentina squad in Possession Game; existing players keep their chosen squads.
7. Added ML Sprint as a BETA experience. This mode still has many known issues and will continue to receive fixes.
8. Fixed the player draw animation so draw effects follow the actual card type, including BT, Epic and Show Time.
9. Added more DIY players to their DIY pack, player inventory and GP player signing catalogue.
10. Added this week's two packs; their players are now in newest.

## Upgrading

The package name and signing key are unchanged. Install over the existing app to keep your saves; do not uninstall it or clear its data. An internet connection is required to prepare the selected patch and new resources for this version. Once all required resources are ready, the game can start offline. Interrupted downloads can resume.
