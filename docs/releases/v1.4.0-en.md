## Upgrading and resources

- The APK download location stays the same. Version 1.3.2 can obtain the 1.4 APK through its existing channel, with existing saves retained.
- New resources move to a separate repository with encrypted distribution. Version 1.3.2 can still obtain the 1.4 APK from the existing download location. Upgrade to 1.4 to download and read the new resources; the old resource channel will not point to the new repository.
- The download/update page switches between Chinese and English and remembers the last choice. Transient network failures retry automatically, with completed download progress retained.
- Choose a patch at each launch, or use Continue with the last successful choice. Switching handles the previous patch's exclusive files while retaining saves and shared resources.
- Champions League and Premier League patches are included. Original assets have been received and need kit/badge compatibility work; World Cup assets are pending.
- Android 9 is the new minimum, on 64-bit ARM devices. Actual compatibility remains subject to device testing.

## Player editor and skills

- Open the editor from an owned player's action menu, including the Game Plan player entry. It uses a full-page settings layout; backing out does not save. Celebration editing has been removed.
- Raise the level cap up to 100, adjust weak-foot accuracy and usage, and add trainable booster slots, with at most two total boosters.
- Changes apply to the selected copy. Other versions and copies remain separate. Official fixed boosters, base abilities, preferred left/right foot and innate skills are not directly editable.
- Reset restores the level, experience and cap from before editor use, removes progression allocations and returns their points, and restores original booster slots, innate skills and weak-foot values. Learned additional skills remain; editor-added slots and their effects are removed.
- Additional skill slots increase from five to ten, with training, saving and match use. Detail pages can display up to 100 skill entries.
- Trending cards can train additional skills while retaining their fixed level 1 rule. Recommended progression continues to use the game's native allocation.
- Select a booster type, then an available strength, with one final confirmation. The blue i retains the name and actual effect description; the invalid ability-comparison section is removed.

## Inventory, search and Game Plans

- Name search is added to the main inventory, player GP shop, and the player picker opened beneath the Game Plan substitutes. Chinese and English are supported, including case, accents and common punctuation differences.
- Search positioning follows card/list view changes, moving into the empty right-hand area in card view.
- Player rows are created in batches, prioritizing the first roughly 200 entries. Reduces repeated rebuilding when opening inventories or returning from player details.
- Unchanged lists retain sorting and scroll position. Training and saved edits still trigger the required refresh.
- Up to 20 Game Plans can be saved, retaining existing plans, the main plan and squads.
- Initial inventory is limited to BT, ST, Epic and all registered Messi versions instead of approximately 14,000 cards. Upgrade cleanup first backs up the old save, then removes other copies only when no Game Plan references them and they are confirmed never to have played. Unknown statistics are retained.
- Player and manager GP shops have separate complete available catalogues. Username, existing avatar and background selections persist after restarting. The existing unlimited, constant currency behavior remains.

## Matches, divisions and other improvements

- AI Divisions, My League and other supported AI entries share native Match Settings, including available duration, injuries, extra time, penalties, substitution limits and team condition choices.
- Adds the native Player match camera and addresses camera choices that did not actually switch the match view.
- Expands existing balls, stadiums, TIFOs, entrance/goal effects, turf, nets and advertising options, and fixes saved stadium settings being overwritten at kickoff.
- Makes 61 resource-backed special kits available, including holiday and event designs, with manual choices retained. Keeps 90/120 fps options and saved settings, with localization adjustments.
- AI Division match history works again. Each skip advances one division, up to Division 3; retrying the same request does not skip again.
- Fixes located My League, team replacement and bundle issues, and restores correct localized manager titles.
- Home feature banners present the added features and open their own detailed bilingual in-game guide.
- Player details support A–E Live Update condition. Alignment with the official current week remains under review and will continue to be improved.
- Improves local agreement confirmation, login-free startup, Google framework compatibility and old-save checks, reducing external authentication waits while retaining error details and feedback tools.
